Our Heritage

Built on Precision, Honesty & Local Trust

Located centrally at Ji. Pra. Ka. Chowk, Gulariya, Ali Mobile Center & Apple Fix started as a focused electronics repair bench over a decade ago. As smartphones transformed into modern pocket computers holding people’s livelihoods, banking, and precious memories, we invested heavily into specialized micro-soldering equipment, cleanroom work surfaces, and genuine manufacturer supply channels.

Today, we operate Bardiya's premier dedicated Apple Fix laboratory alongside our official smartphone showroom. We take pride in diagnosing problems right in front of you, quoting fair prices before taking apart a device, and safeguarding customer privacy above all else.

Professional Hardware Equipment on Our Bench

We invest in industrial-grade diagnostic equipment so your premium smartphone is treated with laboratory care.

Stereo Optical Microscope

45x continuous zoom trinocular microscope for precision inspection of 0201 SMD resistors, broken circuit jumper wires, and logic board BGA pads.

Digital Ultrasonic Sonicator

Multi-frequency heated ultrasonic chemical bath specifically designed to dislodge liquid oxidation and electrolytic crystals from water-damaged logic boards.

Programmable Rework & Micro-Pencil

Micro-soldering station with rapid temperature recovery, anti-static ESD earthing, and custom nozzles to protect adjacent motherboard IC components.

Our 4 Core Counter Commitments

How we do business every day at Ji. Pra. Ka. Chowk.

01

100% Data Privacy

We respect customer privacy. Technicians do not browse personal photos, chat apps, or notes. We test hardware using dedicated hardware diagnostics.

02

Zero Hidden Extra Fees

Every quote is provided upfront. If unexpected secondary faults are found during repair, we call and obtain your permission before proceeding.

03

No Counterfeits Sold as OEM

We clearly distinguish between original-grade parts and value options. We never sell low-grade copy screens claiming they are genuine displays.
