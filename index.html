Cracked Screen First-Aid

Do not apply hard pressure on glass cracks to avoid bleeding the underlying OLED panel. Tape over cracks with clear tape to prevent finger cuts, and back up your data.

Phone Dropped in Water?

Turn off device immediately. Never plug in a charging cable, and avoid shaking or using a hair dryer. Bring it to our counter within 2 hours for ultrasonic de-oxidation.
